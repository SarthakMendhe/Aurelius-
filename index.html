— The Maison

A century of silent mastery.

Founded in the quiet ateliers of Geneva in 1897, AURELIUS has dedicated five generations to a single pursuit: timepieces that outlive their owners.

Every component is conceived, finished, and signed by hand. From the perlage of a mainplate to the polishing of a single bevel, no detail leaves the bench until it bears the maker's mark.
